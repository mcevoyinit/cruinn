The problem

A recording is not a usable record.

Plenty of important conversations get recorded. Far fewer ever get written down properly. Getting from audio to a record people can rely on is a job of its own, and it usually only gets done when someone needs it in a hurry.

Recorded, then shelved

most audio is never typed up

Meetings, interviews and reviews are recorded all the time. Very few of those recordings ever become something you could quote from.

Bought one job at a time

priced per job, back in days

Transcripts are usually ordered one recording at a time, and then you wait. Cost and delay end up deciding what gets written down and what stays on a disc.

No proof it hasn't changed

in an ordinary transcript

A typed document shows you the words. It can't show you that nobody went back and edited a line.

There's usually a deadline too. Nobody wants a transcript for its own sake. Someone is waiting on it, often to a timetable they don't set. So cost and turnaround matter more than they look. They decide which recordings become records at all.

Why a seal

The recording is protected. The transcript usually isn't.

Organisations that take a recording seriously already look after the original. It's labelled, stored safely and never edited. The transcript is a different story. It's the thing people actually read and quote, and it's usually just a document anyone could open and change. Cruinn gives the transcript the same protection as the recording.

What happens to the recording

The original is kept safe and left alone, and people work from a copy. If anyone ever asks whether it was tampered with, the original settles it.

What Cruinn adds

We seal the transcript too. Once a person has checked and certified it, the text is locked, and every step that led there is logged. Change a single character later and it shows. Anyone can check this for themselves, offline, without asking us.

We never touch your original recording. It stays the master copy of what was said. What we give you is a readable, certified version of it that can prove it hasn't been changed.

How it works

From recording to sealed transcript

Every job follows the same path. Our software writes a quick first draft, a trained person checks every line, and the finished transcript is sealed so any later change shows up.

  1. 1

    Received and logged

    Your recording arrives over a secure link and is fingerprinted the moment it lands. Its history starts there.

  2. 2

    First draft

    Our own speech software, on our own machines in Ireland, writes the first draft. It's tuned for Irish accents.

  3. 3

    Checked by a person

    A vetted reviewer listens through, fixes the draft and certifies every line against the audio.

  4. 4

    Sealed

    The certified text is locked with a cryptographic seal, and every step is written to a log that can only be added to.

  5. 5

    Copies to share

    Every copy you pass on carries the same seal, so whoever gets it can check it.

  6. 6

    Checkable for good

    Anyone can check the seal offline, without us. It still works if we're not around.

Common questions

Questions, answered plainly

Where is our data processed and stored?

In Ireland, always. Recordings and transcripts are processed and stored on machines in Ireland. Your audio is never sent to an outside service or someone else's cloud.

Does software decide what the transcript says?

No. Our software writes a quick first draft on our own machines, but it doesn't certify anything. A vetted reviewer checks every line against the audio and signs it off, so the final text is their judgement.

What happens if someone edits a transcript later?

The seal breaks. Change a single character in a sealed transcript and it no longer matches its seal, so anyone who checks will see it, whoever made the change.

What kinds of recordings do you take?

Interviews, meetings, panels and reviews: anything where the recording needs to stay private and the transcript needs to hold up later. Recorded interviews are what we do today. If you have something else in mind, ask us.

Can we check the seal without you?

Yes, offline and on your own. Checking compares the transcript with its sealed record using standard, published methods. You don't need to contact us, and it still works if we're no longer around.

Do you change our original recording?

No. We work from a copy and leave your original exactly as it is. It stays the master record of what was said. What we send back is a transcript with a seal of its own.

What isn't built yet?

Transcribing recorded interviews is live. Handwritten pages are in development and two language recordings are on the roadmap, both marked as such above, along with key moment markers and the index. Formal certifications are under way and listed at their real status. We'd rather be dull about what exists than have you find a gap later.
